A short interactive film about late nights with a radio dongle, a homelab full of GPUs, a week on red team engagements, a first Tai Chi class and a very fat cat in round galaxy glasses. Everything you see and hear is generated live in your browser: there are no video or audio files, just canvas drawing and a Web Audio trance score that builds the longer you watch.

**[Open it full screen ↗](/mycelial-nodes/)** (recommended, with sound on).

Press **Jack in** to start. Click, tap or use → to move through the scenes, ← to go back, **M** to mute and **A** to toggle auto-advance. If your system is set to reduce motion, the text appears straight away and the scenes wait for you instead of advancing on their own.

The soundtrack that inspired it is [Super 8 by Coccolino Deep](https://youtu.be/fVH8uiFI0Zs). Open it in another tab if you want it playing underneath.
